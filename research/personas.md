# Primary Persona – Student User

## Overview
Name: Linda  
Age: 21  
Background: University student in Cameroon, studying business.  
Income habits: Monthly allowance (~30,000 CFA), occasional side hustle.

## Pain Points
- Hard to track allowance spending.
- Struggles to save for tuition or personal goals.
- Peer pressure leads to overspending.
- No simple tool tailored for students.

## Goals
- Save at least 5,000 CFA monthly.
- Gain visibility into spending habits.
- Build financial discipline before graduation.
- Use a trustworthy, easy app that feels “made for students.”

## UX Success Criteria
### Must feel easy
- Adding allowance or side income.
- Recording daily expenses.
- Starting small savings goals.
- Viewing a simple dashboard.

### Must feel motivating
- Seeing progress toward savings goals.
- Nudges or reminders to save.
- Peer challenges or gamified streaks.
- Clear feedback on weekly savings.



# Secondary Persona – Side-Hustle Student

## Overview
Name: Daniel  
Age: 23  
Background: Computer science student in Cameroon, final year.  
Income habits: Runs a side hustle (freelance coding + phone repairs), irregular income.

## Pain Points
- Hard to manage irregular income.
- Struggles to separate business money from personal spending.
- Often runs out of cash before month’s end.
- No clear savings plan for graduation or future projects.

## Goals
- Save consistently for graduation expenses and startup ideas.
- Track allowance and side-hustle income separately.
- Build discipline to avoid mixing business and personal money.
- Use a tool that works offline and is simple to update quickly.

## UX Success Criteria
### Must feel easy
- Logging irregular income.
- Separating business vs personal expenses.
- Starting savings goals tied to future projects.
- Quick updates on mobile with low data use.

### Must feel motivating
- Clear separation of business and personal money.
- Progress bars toward graduation/startup savings.
- Weekly reminders to save.
- Feeling in control despite irregular income.
