# Primary Persona – Student User

## Overview
Name: Linda  
Age: 21  
Background: University student in Cameroon, studying business.  
Income habits: Monthly allowance (~30,000 CFA), occasional side hustle.

## Pain Points
- Hard to track allowance spending.
- Struggles to save for tuition or personal goals.
- Peer pressure leads to overspending.
- No simple tool tailored for students.

## Goals
- Save at least 5,000 CFA monthly.
- Gain visibility into spending habits.
- Build financial discipline before graduation.
- Use a trustworthy, easy app that feels “made for students.”

## UX Success Criteria
### Must feel easy
- Adding allowance or side income.
- Recording daily expenses.
- Starting small savings goals.
- Viewing a simple dashboard.

### Must feel motivating
- Seeing progress toward savings goals.
- Nudges or reminders to save.
- Peer challenges or gamified streaks.
- Clear feedback on weekly savings.
